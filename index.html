    M.A. in Public Policy

    The M.A. in Public Policy (Online), offered by the Jindal School of Government and Public Policy (JSGP) at O.P. Jindal Global University, is a fully online, interdisciplinary master’s programme designed for learners seeking advanced expertise in governance, development, and policy analysis. The curriculum integrates economics, political science, ethics, sociology, and evidence-based policy design, enabling students to build strong analytical and research competencies essential for understanding and shaping public systems in India and the Global South.

    Guided by distinguished JSGP faculty and supported by flexible learning pathways, the programme allows learners to customise their academic journey through a wide selection of electives and credit-eligible industry certifications. Whether aspiring for careers in government, public administration, development organisations, consulting, or policy research, graduates emerge with the intellectual depth, methodological clarity, and professional readiness required to lead and influence the public policy landscape.

    How does the M.A. in Public Policy at JGU Stand Apart?

    Feature JGU – M.A. in Public Policy (Online) Regular Public Policy Programmes
    Institutional Reputation Delivered by Jindal School of Government & Public Policy (JSGP), one of India’s leading public policy schools with strong Global South research credentials. Often follow Western frameworks or theoretical models; limited contextual focus on emerging economies.
    Curriculum Orientation Interdisciplinary curriculum tailored to India + Global South realities, blending governance, development, and applied policy research. Primarily Western-centric frameworks that may not fully address developing-country contexts.
    Mode of Delivery Fully Online, with live sessions, interactive discussions, and applied assessments offering flexibility without academic compromise. Many online programmes are pre-recorded, lecture-heavy, or lack meaningful faculty engagement.
    Elective Flexibility 12+ specialised electives + cross electives from International Relations; highly customisable academic pathways. Limited electives, fewer customisation options, and minimal cross-disciplinary choices.
    Industry Certifications Integrated access to Coursera Career Stack Licenses + Google, IBM, Tableau & Fractal certifications. Industry-aligned certifications are rarely embedded into the curriculum.
    Credit Waivers Up to 10 academic credits waived through approved industry certifications (data analytics + policy design pathways). Credit waivers for external certifications are uncommon or not offered.
    Skills Development Balanced focus on theory + applied skills such as policy design, data analysis, impact evaluation, and governance analytics. Certifications may lack international recognition and practical applicability.
    Faculty Expertise Taught by renowned JSGP scholars and practitioners involved in real-time policy research and governance work. Faculty may have strong academic credentials but limited practitioner grounding in emerging-economy policy issues.
    Career Orientation Designed for roles in government, NGOs, think tanks, consulting, international development, and governance reform. Career preparation varies; many programmes emphasise academic research rather than applied policy careers.
    Audience Fit Ideal for working professionals, career-shifters, and early-career graduates looking for flexible yet rigorous training. Some programmes require full-time commitment or lack flexibility for mid-career learners.
